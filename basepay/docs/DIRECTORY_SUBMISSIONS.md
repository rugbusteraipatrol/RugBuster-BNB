## Description (medium, ~500 characters)

> StableCart is a non-custodial checkout for USDC on the Base network. The buyer
> pays your wallet directly, in one blockchain transaction — StableCart never
> holds the money and cannot freeze, delay or reverse it.
>
> Use it two ways: a WooCommerce plugin that sets up in about a minute by pasting
> a wallet address, or a payment link that needs no website at all. Free for 20
> paid orders a month; Pro is $12 per 30 days. No cut of your sales, ever.

## Description (long)

> StableCart lets a shop take USDC — a dollar stablecoin — on Base, and keeps the
> money non-custodial from end to end. The buyer pays the merchant's own wallet
> in a single transaction. There is no processor account to open, no application
> to be turned down from, no identity check, and no payout schedule, because
> nobody stands between the buyer and the merchant.
>
> Setup is one field. Paste the wallet address that should receive payments and
> save; the shop registers itself with the service. The buyer gets a pay page
> with the exact amount and a QR code that any wallet app can scan, and the order
> completes by itself once the network confirms.
>
> Each checkout is quoted a unique amount, down to fractions of a cent, which is
> how payments are matched to orders without generating a wallet per order.
> Payments that arrive after the quote expires are still matched for 24 hours,
> so a slow buyer does not become a support ticket.
>
> Free for 20 paid orders per calendar month. Pro removes the limit for $12 per
> 30 days, paid in USDC through StableCart itself. Neither plan takes a
> percentage of sales.

## Indie Hackers post (when two directories are live)

Title:

> I built a WooCommerce plugin that takes USDC straight to the merchant's wallet — no processor in between

Body:

> Card processors decline whole categories and whole countries. I wanted the
> simplest possible answer for a small shop: the buyer pays a dollar stablecoin
> directly to the shop's own wallet, and the order completes by itself.
>
> That is StableCart. A WooCommerce plugin, or a payment link if you have no
> website. You paste a wallet address and save — no account, no API key, no
> identity check, because there is nothing to hold. The money never touches my
> server; I only watch the chain and tell your shop when a payment lands.
>
> The part I am proudest of is the boring part: matching a payment to an order.
> Instead of generating a wallet per order, every checkout is quoted a unique
> amount — $25.000173 rather than $25.00 — and that is what identifies it. Late
> payments are still matched for 24 hours after the page expires.
>
> Free for 20 paid orders a month, $12 per 30 days after that, no cut of sales.
> The plugin is in review at WordPress.org; the payment links work today.
>
> What I would like feedback on: is a monthly fee the right shape for this, or
> should it be a one-off? Taking a percentage is off the table — it would mean
> touching the money.
