When NOWPayments is the better fit

Be honest with yourself about what your buyers use. If they pay in Bitcoin, Monero or dozens of altcoins, or you need your sales converted and sent to a bank, a multi-coin processor does things StableCart deliberately does not. StableCart does one thing: USDC on Base, straight to you.

Questions

Can I run StableCart next to NOWPayments?

Yes. They are separate WooCommerce payment methods, so you can offer USDC through StableCart and keep NOWPayments for other coins.

Does StableCart ever hold my money?

No. The buyer pays your wallet directly. StableCart only watches the Base network and tells your shop when the payment arrived.

What happens after 20 orders on the free plan?

New checkouts pause until the next calendar month, or until you buy Pro for $12 per 30 days in the plugin settings. Pro is not a recurring subscription.
