1. What the service does

StableCart watches the Base blockchain for USDC payments made to a wallet address you provide, and reports the status of those payments to you — through a checkout page, a webhook to your website, or a plugin.

2. What the service does not do

  • It never takes custody of money. Payments go from the buyer's wallet to your wallet in a single blockchain transaction.
  • It holds no private keys and cannot move, freeze, reverse or refund a payment.
  • It is not a bank, a payment institution, an exchange or a money transmitter, and it does not convert between currencies.
