How it works

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a merchant account?

No. There is no account to open and no application to be approved. You need a wallet address on the Base network and a WooCommerce shop on a public https address. That is the complete list.

Do I need to pass KYB or business verification?

Not to take a payment. Money moves from your customer's wallet to yours without an intermediary, so there is no underwriter deciding whether your business qualifies.

My business was rejected by Stripe. Will this work?

Most likely, because nobody is reviewing you. Do check that what you sell is legal where you and your customers are — being outside the card networks does not put you outside the law, and that is your responsibility, not something StableCart can check for you.

Do you hold my money at any point?

No. Funds go from the buyer's wallet to yours in a single blockchain transaction. StableCart holds no private key and cannot move, freeze or withhold your money. It reads public blockchain data and reports payment status.

What does it cost?

Free for 20 paid orders a month. Pro is $12 per 30 days for unlimited orders, paid in USDC from the plugin settings, with no subscription to cancel. There is no setup fee and no cut of your sales on either plan.

Your buyer pays the Base network fee to send the payment, typically a fraction of a cent.

Which cryptocurrencies and networks are supported?

USDC on Base, and nothing else — deliberately. USDC is a stablecoin pegged to the dollar, so a $45 order is worth $45 when it settles. Base keeps fees low enough that small payments make sense.

Can customers who have no crypto still pay?

Not at the moment. A buyer needs USDC on Base in a wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet. Someone else can pay on their behalf: what counts is the exact amount arriving, not who sends it.

How long does a payment take to confirm?

Usually under a minute. The pay page shows the buyer live status — waiting, confirming, received — and the order completes on its own.

What if a buyer pays after the pay page expired?

It still counts. An exact payment that arrives up to 24 hours after the quote expired settles the order, as long as no newer order is holding the same amount.

What about refunds and chargebacks?

There are no chargebacks: payments on Base are final once confirmed. Refunds are sent by you, from your own wallet — StableCart holds no keys, which is the same property that stops anyone from freezing your funds. The buyer's address is in the order note.

Does it work outside WooCommerce?

The checkout widget is a single script tag, so it works on any page where you can paste HTML — Webflow, Framer or plain HTML — for “buy this item” buttons. The one-minute setup is WooCommerce-only for now.

How do I know a payment is real?

Every confirmed payment includes its transaction hash, which you can check on a public block explorer. You never have to take our word for it: the chain is the record.
